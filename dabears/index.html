Da Bears

I am a die hard Bears fan. I’ve been following the team closely since we drafted Mitchell Trubisky. I remained a Fields truther throughout 2023 (yes, I thought we should draft MHJ and build around Fields). Although the last decade hasn’t been easy, Caleb and Ben are bringing a new era to Chicago. Two of my favorite memories were watching the DJ Moore walkoff TD against the Pack with my family and friends, and watching the Kmet TD against the Rams in New Zealand with my Dad.
