Hi, I’m John

Hi! I’m John Bezaitis, a senior at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign studying Economics and Statistics. Previously, I worked at Golub Capital, one of the largest private credit firms in the United States, as an Operations Intern. I’m currently recruiting for full-time roles.

On campus, I serve as a Senior Analyst in the University of Illinois Investment Management Academy, a student-run portfolio of $1.5 million benchmarked against the S&P 600. I’m also the President of the Euchre League, a card-playing organization that brings together over 100 members each week around the game of Euchre. Outside of those roles, I volunteer as a mentor with the Illini Mentor Program, an afterschool initiative supporting local elementary school students.

When I’m not on campus, you can find me sailing, playing cards, traveling, or cheering on the Chicago Bears. I also have a dog named Poppy (arguably the best puppy ever) and you can check out some photos of her here.

Thanks for stopping by! Be sure to explore my trips page to see what I’ve been up to lately, along with some exciting plans on the horizon.
