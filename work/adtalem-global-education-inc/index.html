Overview

Adtalem Global Education Inc. (Adtalem, The Company) is a for profit education provider specializing in training nurses, veterinarians, and professionals in other healthcare-related fields. Adtalem operates through three main institutions: Chamberlain University, the largest nursing school in the United States, Walden University, and three medical and veterinary schools. The company largely generates revenue from student tuition and fees, and is heavily reliant on Title IV funding, which provides students a way to fund their education. Adtalem has seen major success in recent quarters, demolishing enrollment records, and achieving revenue growth for the last five years straight. Building on this trajectory, the company is positioned for a strong FY 2026 as momentum continues across its portfolio of institutions. This growth is guided by its “Growth with Purpose” strategy, which implies horizontal expansion into in-demand healthcare education markets and operational excellence. Adtalem offers a low cost, online education alternative compared to traditional universities.
