About Me

I am a student with a passion for finance, data analytics, and education. I love working at the intersection of all three fields, where I can combine my soft skills in education with my hard skills built by my coursework and extracurriculars.

With a background in economics and statistics, I’ve been able to leverage my experience to real-world applications like the Investment Management Academy and Golub Capital. Today, I’m looking to pursue my next opportunity to get a better grasp on what the world around me has to offer.

My curiosity, energy, and desire to explore are traits that define me. I take every opportunity I can to say ‘yes’, and I’m always looking for the next break in my schedule to do something new.

I bring this mentality to my work, grounded in the belief that communication and stepping up to every opportunity are the most important drivers of a successful team. Ensuring every idea is heard, leveraging people’s strengths, and establishing a clear plan of action are essential to delivering high-quality work on time. It’s no different from scaling the Andes deep in the Peruvian forest with a hiking group. Making sure everyone is equipped with supplies, routes are mapped, and morale stays high are all fundamental to putting one foot in front of the other.

I’m always looking for the next opportunity or trip to plan. If you’re interested in connecting, or just want to share stories about the latest adventure, I’d love to hear from you!
